I believe that impactful science extends beyond the laboratory bench into mentorship, student advocacy, and inclusive scientific outreach. Throughout my academic training and continuing in the community, I have actively led graduate student organizations, mentored emerging researchers, and volunteered with youth educational programs.

## Professional Service & Academic Leadership

### Department of Cellular Biology & Graduate School
* **President**, Cellular Biology Graduate Student Association (2022 – 2023)
  * Facilitated constructive dialogue between graduate students and departmental leadership during town hall meetings.
  * Successfully negotiated and secured departmental funding ($800) for graduate student professional development at UGA Career Day.
  * Organized department-wide community-building events for graduate students, faculty, and research staff.
  * Introduced targeted initiatives supporting student well-being and career growth, including Student Wellness Days, Write-in Happy Hours, and Data Visualization workshops.
* **Cell Biology Department Graduate Council Representative** (2021 – 2022)
  * Advocated on behalf of graduate and professional students in campus council meetings, addressing student welfare, campus resources, and academic policies.
* **Social Chair**, Cellular Biology Graduate Student Association (2020 – 2021)
  * Launched the "Science Talk Space" initiative, providing graduate students an informal, peer-driven forum to present and discuss emerging scientific topics.
  * Coordinated welcoming social events for incoming and returning departmental cohorts.

### Developmental Biology Alliance
* **Co-President**, Developmental Biology Alliance Graduate Student Association (2022 – 2023)
  * Planned and executed the annual Developmental Biology Alliance Spring Retreat and Fall Symposium.
  * Recruited and secured four renowned keynote speakers from leading institutions across the country.
* **Social Chair**, Developmental Biology Alliance Graduate Student Association (2021 – 2022)
  * Organized cross-departmental community-building and holiday networking events connecting students, postdoctoral scholars, and faculty.

## Mentorship & STEM Outreach

* **College Access Program Mentor**, *Ocean Discovery Institute* (San Diego, CA) | Aug 2026 – Present
  * Mentoring high school students to navigate the college admissions journey through college matchmaking, personal statement support, and educational/career goal planning in STEM.
* **Science Communication Volunteer**, *Fleet Science Center* (San Diego, CA) | 2026
  * Engaging the public, students, and families in interactive, hands-on scientific demonstrations and biological education.
* **Undergraduate & Graduate Research Mentor**, *Kamiyama Laboratory, UGA* | 2021 – 2022
  * Supervised and mentored two undergraduate research assistants in project design, advanced imaging techniques, and quantitative analysis.
  * Trained four rotation graduate students and two junior PhD peers in *Drosophila* genetics, super-resolution microscopy sample preparation, and experimental methodology.
* **Research Mentor**, *Nanotechnology and Biomedicine REU* (NSF Research Experiences for Undergraduates) | 2021
  * Trained visiting REU scholar in biological sample preparation and fluorophore optimization for Stochastic Optical Reconstruction Microscopy (STORM).
  * Guided the student through experimental design, quantitative image processing, and scientific presentation, culminating in their presentation at a regional symposium.
* **High School Research Mentor**, *UGA Young Dawgs Program* | 2018 – 2019
  * Mentored high school senior in fine embryonic dissection techniques and confocal microscopy imaging, enabling the student to showcase their findings to an interdisciplinary cohort.
* **Volunteer Youth Tutor**, *Mind Blowers / Boys & Girls Club & East Athens WIT* | 2015 – 2018
  * Provided after-school academic tutoring and mentorship to local youth in Athens, GA.
