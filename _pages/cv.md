Research Experience
======
* **Doctoral Researcher / Research Assistant**, Department of Cellular Biology, University of Georgia (2019 – 2025)
  * *Advisor:* Dr. Daichi Kamiyama
  * Discovered an axon guidance mechanism mediated by a novel cluster of non-synaptic filopodia on *Drosophila* muscles using confocal and super-resolution microscopy.
  * Identified filopodia activity across the musculature using lipophilic dye labeling and fluorescent protein labeling driven by muscle-specific Gal4 drivers.
  * Standardized an in situ 2-color single molecule localization microscopy (SMLM) pipeline and uncovered beads-on-a-string morphology of muscle filopodia in *Drosophila* embryos.
  * Identified Fas2 and Ncad as filopodial adhesion markers by mapping localization patterns of >15 synaptic proteins using split-GFP and immunofluorescence assays in *Drosophila*.

* **Research Technician**, Department of Cellular Biology, University of Georgia (2018 – 2019)
  * *Advisor:* Dr. Daichi Kamiyama
  * Optimized single-color in vivo SMLM pipelines in motor neurons and primary neuron cultures, providing a validated framework for dual-color super-resolution studies.
  * Systematically screened and characterized the expression patterns of 85 Gal4-drivers to enable neuromuscular junction studies across subsets of 30 embryonic muscles.
  * Troubleshot sample preparation and imaging parameters to maximize reproducibility and data quality.

* **Undergraduate Research Assistant**, Department of Biochemistry and Molecular Biology, University of Georgia (2016 – 2017)
  * *Advisor:* Dr. Hang Yin
  * Developed a cost-effective tissue clearing pipeline (CLARITY) for mouse organs (e.g., muscle, brain), enabling 3D volumetric deep-tissue analysis.
  * Performed microsurgical extraction of mouse myofibers and isolated/cultured satellite cells for high-resolution imaging and adipogenic transdifferentiation studies.

Skills & Technical Expertise
======
* **Assay Development & Optimization**: Cell-based & functional assay design, reporter validation, flow cytometry/FACS characterization, transfection, and assay optimization.
* **Advanced Light & Super-Resolution Microscopy**: Hands-on expertise with STORM, PALM, SIM, confocal, and live-cell imaging; custom microscope design, alignment, and optimization.
* **Complex Sample Prep & Tissue Processing**: Development of tissue clearing pipelines (e.g., CLARITY) for 3D volumetric deep-tissue analysis; cell/tissue culture of mammalian and insect lines (HeLa, HEK293, U2OS, S2) and primary cells (myofibers, satellite cells).
* **High-Fidelity Immunofluorescence (IF)**: Optimization of sample prep, signal preservation, and signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) in thick tissue samples (20–50 µm) and cell culture.
* **Bioimage Informatics & Computational Analysis**: Quantitative image processing and data analysis using ImageJ/Fiji, Python, and R.
* **Standardization & Documentation**: Experience authoring standardized SOPs, publishing peer-reviewed experimental protocols, and managing reproducible data records.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration & Leadership**: Interdisciplinary teamwork (partnering with optics engineers), student mentorship, and graduate association leadership.
