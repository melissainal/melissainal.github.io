### Overview & Significance
By establishing targeted binary Gal4/LexA driver lines, this work reveals that individual embryonic muscles execute divergent postsynaptic morphogenetic and membrane remodeling strategies during target innervation. This work directly informs my interest in resolving cell-type-specific subcellular specializations and provides a versatile translational platform for high-content functional screening and modeling muscle-specific vulnerability in neuromuscular disease.
