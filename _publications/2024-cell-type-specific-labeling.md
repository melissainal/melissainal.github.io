### Overview & Significance
This work establishes an optimized methodology for cell-type-specific labeling of endogenous proteins using split-GFP reconstitution in intact tissues, circumventing antibody penetration limitations in deep specimens. This approach is central to my future goals of mapping nanoscale protein architecture across multicellular interfaces, providing powerful translational utility for therapeutic target validation, endogenous biomarker tracking, and assay development.
