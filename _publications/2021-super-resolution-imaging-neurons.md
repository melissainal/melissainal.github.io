### Overview & Significance
This protocol details an optimized Single-Molecule Localization Microscopy (STORM) pipeline achieving ~20-nm optical resolution in primary neuronal cultures and intact embryonic tissues. Overcoming light scattering and photoblinking constraints in thick samples underpins my drive to build multiplexed super-resolution imaging technologies, with high translational value for nanometer-scale pathology, drug-target engagement mapping, and structural biomarker discovery.
