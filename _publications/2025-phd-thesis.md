### Overview & Significance
This dissertation provides a multiscale characterization of filopodial interactions guiding neuromuscular circuit assembly, uniting in vivo super-resolution STORM microscopy, split-GFP protein topography, and live imaging to uncover novel muscle-driven guidance mechanisms. This body of work establishes the conceptual and methodological foundation for my future research into subcellular nanodomain signaling, while delivering imaging pipelines with direct translational application to neuromuscular disease therapeutics.
