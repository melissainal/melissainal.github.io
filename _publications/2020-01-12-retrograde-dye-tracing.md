### Overview & Significance
We standardized a robust protocol for single- and multi-color retrograde labeling of embryonic motor neurons using lipophilic fluorescent dyes, permitting high-fidelity visualization of fine neurites and exploratory filopodia. This methodology provided a foundation for my work on contact-mediated circuit assembly and offers a valuable translational paradigm for tract tracing, evaluating axon regeneration integrity, and assessing synaptic connectivity in injury models.
